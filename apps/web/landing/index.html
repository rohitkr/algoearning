Features

Everything from idea to live order, in one place

Strategy builder

Multi-leg options strategies with strikes picked by distance from ATM or by premium. Add per-leg stop-losses, targets, trailing and re-entries. No code needed.

Backtests on real history

Replay a strategy over stored index and option candles, using the same code that trades live. You get the full trade list, daily P&L and drawdown.

Paper first, then live

Every strategy runs on live prices with simulated fills first. When you trust it, switch it to your Zerodha account with one click.

SMC options scalper

Smart Money Concepts turned into objective rules: market structure, liquidity sweeps and displacement. It buys calls or puts, intraday only.

Risk controls built in

Daily loss and profit limits, square-off times, position reconciliation with your broker, and a kill switch that closes everything.

Alerts where you are

Entries, exits, stop-losses, failed orders and broker logins, sent to Telegram or email. You choose which.

How it works

Three steps to your first automated trade

  1. 1

    Connect Zerodha

    Add your Kite Connect API key once. It is encrypted at rest and never shown again. Each morning you log in to Zerodha, as usual.

  2. 2

    Build and test

    Start from a template or build your own. Backtest it on history, then run it on paper.

  3. 3

    Go live

    Deploy on your account. Watch positions and P&L live, and stop any strategy at any time.

Safety

Built so a bad day stays a small one

Automation should make trading calmer, not riskier. These guardrails are part of the platform and can’t be skipped.

  • Your money stays with your broker. AlgoEarning places orders; it never holds funds.
  • Every order is placed only once. Orders are tagged and tracked, so a retry never duplicates a position.
  • Positions are checked against Zerodha. A mismatch alerts you immediately.
  • Limits apply per strategy and per day. A breach squares off and stops the strategy.
  • Your data is walled off. The database itself keeps every user’s data private.
